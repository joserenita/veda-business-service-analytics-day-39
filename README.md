## 🚀 Key Business Insights
1. **Acquisition Channels:** High-intent channels such as LinkedIn and Direct Referrals generate a significantly higher client conversion rate.
2. **Feature Engineering:** Composite engagement metrics (`engagement_score` and `inquiry_intensity`) successfully isolate high-probability clients.
